50+ Best Midjourney Prompts for Stunning Visuals

By Sarah Jenkins • Updated Feb 1, 2026

Generative AI has changed the world of design forever. But the secret to getting great results isn't the AI itself—it's the prompt. Below is our curated collection of over 50 tested prompts for Midjourney v6, categorized for easy use.

Use these to create lifelike human characters.

/imagine prompt: A futuristic cyberpunk street food vendor, neon lights, rain-slicked streets, cinematic lighting, bokeh effect, hyper-realistic --ar 16:9
/imagine prompt: Close-up of a blue eye, iris details, macro photography, studio lighting, sharp focus, 8k resolution --style raw
/imagine prompt: Fashion photography shot of a woman wearing avant-garde plastic clothing, desert background, harsh sunlight, high contrast --ar 2:3
/imagine prompt: Candid shot of a laughing child in a park, golden hour lighting, motion blur background, authentic emotion, Nikon D850

💡 Pro Tip: Midjourney Parameters
• --ar 16:9 : Sets aspect ratio to widescreen.
• --v 6.0 : Forces Version 6 (best quality).
• --style raw : Reduces Midjourney's default "beautification" for more realistic results.
• --no text : Tells the AI to avoid generating text.

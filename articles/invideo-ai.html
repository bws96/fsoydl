1. What Makes InVideo AI Different?

Most "AI video tools" are just stock footage libraries with a timeline. InVideo AI is different because it is a generative end-to-end platform.

When you type "Create a 5-minute documentary about the history of Bitcoin," it doesn't just search for a clip. It:

  • Synthesizes a Script: It scrapes the web for accurate info and structures it into a narrative.
  • Generates Voiceovers: It uses advanced TTS (Text-to-Speech) that mimics human inflection.
  • Sources B-Roll: It pulls from a massive library of premium stock (iStock, Shutterstock) to match every sentence.

2. Feature Deep Dive: The "Magic Box"

The core of InVideo AI is the "Magic Box" editor. Instead of cutting clips on a timeline, you edit by talking to the AI. It feels like giving feedback to a human editor.

3. Tutorial: How to Write the Perfect Prompt

The quality of your video depends 100% on your initial prompt. Don't just say "Make a video about cats." Use this formula:

[Topic] + [Duration] + [Platform] + [Tone] + [Specific Visuals]

Bad Prompt: "Make a video about productivity."

Good Prompt: "Create a 3-minute YouTube video about 'The Pomodoro Technique' for college students. Use an energetic, fast-paced tone. Include stock footage of students studying and animated clocks. Start with a hook about procrastination."
